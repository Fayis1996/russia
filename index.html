Day-by-Day Expedition Flow

THE 8-DAY ITINERARY

Tailored day-by-day sequence from arrival in Moscow through Murmansk, Teriberka, St Petersburg, and Sapsan return.

1

DAY 1: FLY FROM INDIA → REACH MOSCOW & STAY

Stay at Moscow

Flight from India via Baku. Arrive Moscow, hotel check-in, rest, twilight Red Square & Kremlin walk.

2

DAY 2: FLY TO MURMANSK • LENIN ICEBREAKER • AURORA

Stay at Murmansk

Fly to Murmansk (included). Visit Lenin Nuclear Icebreaker & Alyosha. Night Northern Lights hunting.

3

DAY 3: HUSKY PARK & NORTHERN LIGHTS HUNTING

Stay at Murmansk

Husky dog sledding safari, reindeer feeding, Sami lunch. Evening Northern Lights hunting expedition.

4

DAY 4: TERIBERKA TRIP & STAY AT TERIBERKA

Stay at Teriberka

120 km Arctic drive, Dragon Egg Beach, Arctic Windmill, Arctic seafood lunch (₹1,500/pax), coastal stay.

5

DAY 5: TERIBERKA TO AIRPORT → FLY TO ST PETERSBURG

Stay at St Petersburg

Transfer to Murmansk airport (₹3,000/pax), domestic flight to St Petersburg (included), canal walk & boat ride.

6

DAY 6: ST PETERSBURG SIGHTSEEING & STAY

Stay at St Petersburg

Winter Palace & Hermitage, Church on Spilled Blood, Peter & Paul Fortress, St Isaac's Cathedral.

7

DAY 7: SAPSAN TO MOSCOW • SHOPPING & PURCHASES

Stay at Moscow

250 km/h Sapsan train (₹5,000/pax). Izmailovsky Kremlin artisan market shopping & purchases, dinner.

8

DAY 8: DEPARTURE FLIGHT TO INDIA

In Flight / Homeward

Transfer to Moscow Vnukovo airport. International departure flight back to India via Baku.
