MURMANSK: LENIN ICEBREAKER & AURORA

Fly north above 68°N latitude, visit the legendary Soviet nuclear icebreaker, and hunt for celestial ribbons of the Northern Lights across dark polar skies.

Aurora Borealis dancing over snow-laden pine forests of Murmansk
DAY 2: FLY NORTH & ICEBREAKER Daytime Program
  • Fly to Murmansk: Morning flight Aeroflot SU-1322 (09:25–12:15) crossing into the Arctic Circle. (Included in ₹91,000 package).
